YT Studio Uploader

A private tool used by one creator to upload videos to their own YouTube channels and to keep a Google Sheet of their own content plan. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

The app only accesses the signed-in owner's own YouTube channels (to upload videos, thumbnails and metadata) and the owner's own Google Sheets (to read and write a content tracking sheet).

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data is used only to perform the actions the owner requests.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
